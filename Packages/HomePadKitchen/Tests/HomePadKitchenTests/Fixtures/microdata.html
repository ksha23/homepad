Simple Banana Bread

Moist and easy.

1 hour 1 loaf (10 slices)
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a loaf pan.
  2. Mix the bananas and butter, then the sugar, then the flour.
  3. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a tester comes out clean.
