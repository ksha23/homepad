Quick Pickled Onions

Serves 8. These keep for two weeks in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

Instructions

  1. Pack the onion into a pint jar.
  2. Warm the vinegar, water, sugar and salt until dissolved, about 2 minutes.
  3. Pour over the onion and let stand 30 minutes before serving.

Notes

Add a few peppercorns if you like.
